## Becoming a Maintainer

1. Make sustained contributions to code, reviews, and documentation.
2. Follow the process described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/vdaas/vald/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
3. Get nominated by an existing maintainer and approved by a majority of maintainers.

A maintainer who can no longer fulfill the responsibilities moves to Emeritus Maintainers.
